# EXelta and the shared intelligence architecture

## Product boundaries

- **XIO** is the recovered XIO 2.0 product environment.
- **Zioren** is the companion personal-intelligence ecosystem.
- **EXelta** is the named user-facing AI in both environments.
- **Intelligence Layer** is the shared architecture, not another product competing with XIO or Zioren.
- **Zio Effect** is the long-term compounding advantage generated by relevant context, learning, and verified outcomes.

## Core responsibilities

The Intelligence Layer assembles active context, retrieves eligible memory and source-backed evidence, evaluates relevance and confidence, coordinates NEXUS processing, routes reasoning, applies governance and approval boundaries, and verifies outcomes.

Memory Fabric records retain context, scope, provenance, confidence, relationships, consent, and lifecycle. Dormant memories can be retrieved when their original context returns, but must be re-evaluated before they influence a response or recommendation. Memory remains distinct from belief, pattern, insight, and recommendation. Research evidence does not automatically become memory.

NEXUS connects eligible evidence and work items for downstream analysis. Shadow represents possible futures and alternative scenarios; it does not turn possibilities into facts. Governance remains separate from cognitive capability: EXelta can propose, explain, challenge, and recommend, but does not gain execution authority merely because its reasoning improves.

## Experience principles

The user experience is a spatial intelligence environment rather than a conventional dashboard, widget grid, or generic chatbot. The interface uses fields, nodes, relationships, rings, and transitions to make real system state legible. Motion is tied to actual listening, retrieval, reasoning, verification, approval, or learning; decorative animation must not imply work that did not happen.

The entry experience remains cinematic and deliberate. The workspace should feel like a quiet, premium instrument with cosmic depth beneath the surface—not a sci-fi control panel. It must remain accessible, responsive, and respectful of reduced-motion settings.

## Identity rules

- User-facing assistant name: **EXelta**.
- Product names: **XIO** and **Zioren** remain distinct.
- Technical identifiers and API paths may retain historical names where changing them would break compatibility; those identifiers are not user-facing brand claims.
